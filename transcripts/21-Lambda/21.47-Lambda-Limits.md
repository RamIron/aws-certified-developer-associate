# 21.47 - Lambda Limits

> **Lecture 310** | Section 21: AWS Serverless: Lambda
> All Lambda limits you need to know for the exam (per region)

---

## All limits are PER REGION

---

## Execution Limits

| Limit | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Memory** | 128 MB to 10 GB (1 MB increments) |
| **Max execution time** | 900 seconds (15 minutes) |
| **Environment variables** | 4 KB max |
| **/tmp storage** | Up to 10 GB |
| **Concurrent executions** | 1,000 (can request increase) |

---

## Deployment Limits

| Limit | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Compressed zip** | 50 MB max |
| **Uncompressed** | 250 MB max |
| **Environment variables** | 4 KB max |

> For files larger than 250 MB → use **/tmp** directory at runtime (up to 10 GB)

---

## Exam Decision Table

```
  "We need 30 GB of RAM"           → ❌ NOT Lambda (max 10 GB)
  "We need 30 min execution"       → ❌ NOT Lambda (max 15 min)
  "We need a 3 GB file"            → ✅ Use /tmp (up to 10 GB)
  "Deployment is 300 MB"           → ❌ Exceeds 250 MB uncompressed
                                     → Use container image (up to 10 GB)
  "Need 2,000 concurrent"          → ✅ Request quota increase
```

---

## Exam Tips

- All limits are **per region**
- Memory: **128 MB – 10 GB** in **1 MB increments** (more memory = more vCPU)
- Max timeout: **15 minutes** — anything longer = Fargate/ECS/EC2
- Environment variables: **4 KB** — tiny!
- /tmp: **10 GB** — for large temporary files
- Concurrent: **1,000** default — request increase via support
- Deployment zip: **50 MB compressed / 250 MB uncompressed**
- Need bigger deployments? → **Container images** (up to 10 GB from ECR)
