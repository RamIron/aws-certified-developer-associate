WCU & RCU FormulasFórmulas de WCU y RCU

The most calculation-heavy topic in DynamoDB — know all three read modes coldEl tema con más cálculos en DynamoDB — dominá los tres modos de lectura

WCU — Write Capacity Unit

1 WCU = 1 write/sec for items up to 1 KB — round UP to nearest 1 KB1 WCU = 1 escritura/seg para ítems de hasta 1 KB — redondear hacia ARRIBA al KB más cercano

WCU
WCU = (writes/sec) × ceil(item_KB / 1)
ExampleEjemplo CalculationCálculo ResultResultado
10 writes/sec, 2 KB each10 escrituras/seg, 2 KB 10 × 2 20 WCU
6 writes/sec, 4.5 KB each6 escrituras/seg, 4.5 KB 6 × 5 (4.5 → rounds up to 5)(4.5 → sube a 5) 30 WCU
120 writes/min, 2 KB each120 escrituras/min, 2 KB (120/60) × 2 = 2 × 2 4 WCU

RCU — Read Capacity Unit

1 RCU = 1 strongly consistent read/sec OR 2 eventually consistent reads/sec, for items up to 4 KB — round UP to nearest 4 KB1 RCU = 1 lectura strongly consistent/seg O 2 eventually consistent/seg, para ítems de hasta 4 KB — redondear al múltiplo de 4 KB más cercano hacia ARRIBA

Strongly Consistent
RCU = reads × ceil(KB / 4)
Eventually Consistent (default)
RCU = (reads / 2) × ceil(KB / 4)
Transactional (2× — prepare + commit phases)
RCU = (reads × 2) × ceil(KB / 4)
Transactional WCU: WCU = (writes × 2) × ceil(KB / 1)
Worked example — 8 reads/sec, 6 KB itemsEjemplo — 8 lecturas/seg, ítems de 6 KB: 6 KB rounds UP to 8 KB (nearest multiple of 4)6 KB sube a 8 KB (múltiplo de 4 más cercano)
Mode CalculationCálculo ResultResultado
Strongly Consistent 8 × (8/4) = 8 × 2 16 RCU
Eventually Consistent (8/2) × (8/4) = 4 × 2 8 RCU
Transactional (8 × 2) × (8/4) = 16 × 2 32 RCU

Optimistic LockingBloqueo Optimista

Add a version attribute to each item. Write with condition version = <version you read>. If someone else modified it first → version mismatch → ConditionalCheckFailedException → re-read and retry.Agregá un atributo version a cada ítem. Write Sharding — Hot Partition FixWrite Sharding — Fix para Hot Partition

Problem: Low-cardinality PKs (e.g. only candidate_a / candidate_b) → hot partitions. Solution: Append a random or hash-calculated suffix to the PK to spread writes. Trade-off: reads must query all shards and aggregate in app code.Problema: PKs de baja cardinalidad (ej: solo candidate_a / candidate_b) → hot partitions. Solución: Agregar un sufijo aleatorio o calculado por hash al PK para distribuir escrituras. Trade-off: las lecturas deben consultar todos los shards y agregar en el código de la app.

Without sharding: candidate_a → Partition 1 (ALL votes) 🔥 candidate_b → Partition 2 (ALL votes) 🔥 With random suffix: candidate_a_11 → Partition 3 ✅ candidate_a_20 → Partition 7 ✅ candidate_b_17 → Partition 1 ✅
