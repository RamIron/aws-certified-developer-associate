# 22.4 - DynamoDB WCU & RCU - Throughput

## Key Concepts

### 1. Capacity Modes Overview

| Mode | Planning | Cost | Throttling | Switch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Provisioned** | Must set RCU + WCU upfront | Pay for what you provision | Yes — `ProvisionedThroughputExceededException` | Can switch once every 24h |
| **On-Demand** | None — unlimited | Pay per request (~2.5x more expensive) | No | Can switch once every 24h |

**When to use On-Demand:** unknown or unpredictable workloads.

---

### 2. Write Capacity Unit (WCU)

> 1 WCU = 1 write/sec for an item up to **1 KB**
> Items larger than 1 KB: **round UP to nearest 1 KB**

**Formula:**
```
WCU = (writes/sec) × ceil(item_size_KB / 1)
```

**Examples:**
| Scenario | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 10 items/sec, 2 KB each | 10 × (2/1) | 20 WCU |
| 6 items/sec, 4.5 KB each | 6 × ceil(4.5) = 6 × 5 | 30 WCU |
| 120 items/min, 2 KB each | (120/60) × (2/1) = 2 × 2 | 4 WCU |

---

### 3. Read Consistency Modes

| Mode | Description | Cost | API parameter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eventually Consistent** (default) | May return stale data right after a write (~100ms lag) | Normal | — |
| **Strongly Consistent** | Always returns latest data | 2× RCU | `ConsistentRead = True` |

Supported by: `GetItem`, `BatchGetItem`, `Query`, `Scan`

---

### 4. Read Capacity Unit (RCU)

> 1 RCU = 1 strongly consistent read/sec, OR 2 eventually consistent reads/sec, for items up to **4 KB**
> Items larger than 4 KB: **round UP to nearest 4 KB**

**Formula:**
```
# Strongly consistent:
RCU = (reads/sec) × ceil(item_size_KB / 4)

# Eventually consistent:
RCU = (reads/sec / 2) × ceil(item_size_KB / 4)
```

**Examples:**
| Scenario | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 10 strongly consistent reads/sec, 4 KB | 10 × (4/4) | 10 RCU |
| 16 eventually consistent reads/sec, 12 KB | (16/2) × (12/4) | 24 RCU |
| 10 strongly consistent reads/sec, 6 KB | 10 × (8/4) ← round 6→8 | 20 RCU |

---

### 5. Partitions & RCU/WCU Distribution

- DynamoDB distributes data across internal partitions using a **hash function** on the partition key
- RCUs and WCUs are **spread evenly across all partitions**
  - e.g. 10 WCU across 10 partitions → each partition gets 1 WCU

> **Important:** Throttling happens at the **partition level**, not the table level.

**Causes of `ProvisionedThroughputExceededException`:**
- Hot key (one partition key read/written too often)
- Hot partition
- Very large items (consume more RCU/WCU per operation)

**Solutions:**
1. Exponential backoff (built into SDK automatically)
2. Better partition key distribution (high cardinality)
3. For read-heavy hot keys → use **DAX** (DynamoDB Accelerator)

---

### 6. On-Demand Mode Details

- Units are called **RRU** (Read Request Units) and **WRU** (Write Request Units)
- Same computation as RCU/WCU — just billed per request instead of per hour
- No throttling, no capacity planning
- ~**2.5x more expensive** than provisioned

---

## Exam Tips

- WCU: 1 KB boundary, round UP
- RCU: 4 KB boundary, round UP; strongly consistent = 2× cost
- `ConsistentRead = True` → strongly consistent (doubles RCU cost)
- `ProvisionedThroughputExceededException` → use exponential backoff
- On-demand = ~2.5x more expensive than provisioned
- Can switch modes once every **24 hours**
- RCU/WCU are split evenly across partitions → hot partitions cause throttling
